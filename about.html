About Maureen

Helping Leaders Focus on What Matters Most

For more than three decades, I've focused on answering one question:

What distinguishes exceptional leaders from everyone else?

That question has shaped every chapter of my career.

I've served as a Chief Human Resources Officer, executive coach, consultant, and trusted advisor to CEOs and senior executives across nonprofit organizations, privately held companies, private equity-backed businesses, and publicly traded organizations.

Along the way I've helped leaders navigate growth, transformation, mergers, organizational change, executive succession, and the everyday realities of leading people.

I later had the privilege of leading an executive advisory practice supporting CHROs from some of the world's most respected organizations while continuing to coach senior executives and leadership teams.

Yet despite countless leadership models, assessments, and frameworks, I kept arriving at the same conclusion:

Leadership is often made more complicated than it needs to be.

Then something unexpected happened.

I learned to play Mahjong.

Around the Mahjong table I saw the same leadership principles I'd spent decades teaching:

  • observation…
  • adaptability…
  • judgment…
  • connection…
  • and helping others succeed.

That unexpected discovery became the foundation for Ancient Game. Modern Leadership.

Today I continue helping executives and leadership teams simplify leadership so they can build stronger organizations and better workplaces.
