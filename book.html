What if one of the world's oldest games held some of leadership's most enduring lessons?

Mahjong rewards preparation, observation, adaptability, emotional discipline, sound judgment, and human connection.

So does leadership.

In Ancient Game. Modern Leadership., Maureen Cahill reveals ten unexpected parallels between the Mahjong table and the leadership table—and translates each one into practical actions leaders can use immediately.
