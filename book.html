Frequently Asked Questions

When does Ancient Game. Modern Leadership. come out, and where can I buy it?

Ancient Game. Modern Leadership. by Maureen A. Cahill releases on October 20, 2026. It will be available in paperback and ebook through Amazon and some independent booksellers. Join the launch list at maureenacahill.com to be notified the day it's available.

Do I need to know how to play Mahjong to read this book?

No. Ancient Game. Modern Leadership. is a leadership book, and Mahjong is the lens rather than the subject. Readers who have never touched a tile follow every chapter without trouble — and will leave the book wanting to learn the game. Readers who play American Mah Jongg will recognize the moments — and probably see their own table differently afterward.

Why Mahjong?

Few games reward observation, adaptability, emotional discipline, strategic thinking, and human connection as consistently as Mahjong does. Four players, incomplete information, a hand that has to change as the table changes — the parallels to leadership are direct and memorable. The game is also having a genuine cultural moment, with American Mah Jongg groups forming in cities across the country. Once you see the parallels, you'll never look at leadership or Mahjong the same way.

What are the ten leadership parallels in the book?

Ancient Game. Modern Leadership. organizes ten leadership practices across four phases — Set the Table, Read the Room, Play Your Hand, and Elevate the Game:

  1. Curating the environment
  2. Learning the written and unwritten rules
  3. Building meaningful rituals
  4. Harnessing observation
  5. Regulating emotions
  6. Pivoting when circumstances change
  7. Balancing offense and defense
  8. Celebrating progress
  9. Learning from better players
  10. Fostering connection

Who is Maureen A. Cahill?

Maureen A. Cahill is an executive coach and leadership advisor with more than 30 years leading Human Resources, advising CEOs and CHROs, and working with organizations across nonprofit, higher education, privately held, private equity-backed, and Fortune 500 environments. She is the author of Ancient Game. Modern Leadership. and the founder of Dogwood Glen, a modern rustic event venue in Bentonville, Arkansas.

Is this a good leadership book for new managers, or is it written for executives?

This book is written for every level of manager — including those who aspire to a leadership role. The ten practices in Ancient Game. Modern Leadership. apply whether you lead a team of two or an organization of thousands. New and emerging managers get a practical starting framework. Experienced executives get language for the instincts they already have and a structure for coaching others.

Is this leadership theory or a practical guide?

Practical, deliberately. Each chapter of Ancient Game. Modern Leadership. combines real leadership stories, an insight from the Mahjong table, perspectives from experienced leaders, reflection questions, and actions you can use the same week you read them.

What makes this different from other leadership books?

Most leadership books introduce a new framework. Ancient Game. Modern Leadership. returns to ten practices that have always mattered — creating the right environment, observing carefully, adapting thoughtfully, regulating emotions, building relationships, and helping other people succeed — and uses an ancient game to make them stick. The Mahjong parallel gives readers a way to remember the principles long after they close the book.

Is the book based on real leadership experience?

Yes. The stories come from more than 30 years leading Human Resources, advising CEOs and CHROs, coaching executives, and working across nonprofit, higher education, privately held, private equity-backed, and Fortune 500 organizations. The book also features ten accomplished leaders across industries, functions, and career stages sharing candid accounts of what leadership actually required of them — the wins, the setbacks, and what they'd do differently.
