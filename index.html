About the Author

Meet Maureen

Leadership has been the common thread throughout my career — as a Chief Human Resources Officer, Chief People Officer, executive advisor, coach, facilitator, author, and adjunct professor. I've partnered with leaders shaping organizations through growth, transformation, mergers, culture change, and uncertainty.

Most recently I served as Senior Managing Partner with McLean & Company, advising Chief Human Resource Officers around the world. Teaching graduate students at Carnegie Mellon University showed me just how powerful practical learning can be.

One question stayed with me: how do we make leadership easier to remember — and easier to practice? That question led me to Mahjong, and to this book.
